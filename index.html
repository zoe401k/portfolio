About

My scientific journey has been guided by a fascination to understand our place in the universe. That curiosity first drew me towards astrophysics and later on, the same questions that lead me to look outward also invited me to look back at our own world. From galaxy scale, I turned to the landscapes of Earth to geography, the study of how people and places are connected across space and time. Today I am able to observe our planet from a unique perspective, uncovering patterns that are often invisible from the ground. This website is a record of that continued exploration by showcasing a collection of projects and discoveries made while searching for new ways to breakdown our remarkable world.
